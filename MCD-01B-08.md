## Documentation Conventions: Advisor Selection / Derived Default / Supplier Capability

Consistent with every other product specification in this project, the following three terms are used throughout this document as **documentation conventions only** — they are not new architectural layers and do not modify MCD-01A §3:

- **Advisor Selection** — a value the advisor actively chooses. Architecturally, this is the Configuration layer (or, where the choice changes construction, the Construction/System layer).
- **Derived Default** — a value the system determines automatically from an Advisor Selection, without a separate question. Architecturally, this is the Accessory Engine and/or Supplier Capability Engine resolving a compatible component or value from an existing Configuration selection.
- **Supplier Capability** — the actual data (dimensions, weights, compatibility, pricing, availability) that makes a Derived Default resolvable. Architecturally, this is the Supplier Capability Engine (MCD-01A §5.9) and the Supplier Capability Library, maintained in MCD-04.

## Scope of This Document

MCD-01B-08 is a **family architecture document**, not a full product specification. It defines only what is genuinely shared across every frame-mounted and window-mounted blind system, regardless of which Blind Technology (Roller, Pleated, Cellular, Dual-Fabric Pleated/Cellular, Aluminium Venetian, Wooden Venetian, Softshade) is inserted into it. It does not contain Blind-Technology-specific rules — those belong in separate variant specifications (MCD-01B-08A onwards), to be drafted later, each referencing this document and its own underlying base specification. It does not contain populated supplier capability records — this document defines the organisational-role *schema* those records will use in MCD-04; it does not populate them. This mirrors exactly how MCD-01B-04 became the Venetian family's reference implementation before Faux Wood and Metal Venetian were drafted, each referencing it rather than duplicating its content, applied here one level up: family architecture, then variants.

1. Scope Statement

This document governs blind systems that mount directly to an individual window or door sash through either:

1. a complete surrounding frame secured through glazing-bead brackets; or
2. top and bottom profiles with independent side guides secured to the window through brackets or adhesive.

The system normally moves with the opening window or door sash. This document governs the mounting-system architecture and its interaction with the inserted Blind Technology. It does not replace the underlying technical specification for Roller, Pleated, Cellular, Aluminium Venetian, or Wooden Venetian products (MCD-01B-01 through MCD-01B-07) — it adds only the rules caused by the frame-mounted/window-mounted construction itself.

2. Construction Groups

Per the Decision Register's Required Classification Question conclusion (three-test reasoning shown there, not restated here as a bare assertion), this family comprises **two Construction Groups**:

- **Full-Frame Bead-Mounted System** — a complete surrounding frame that attaches to the glazing area through compatible glazing-bead brackets (four-sided frame, no wall or recess fixing for the main frame, blind and frame move together with the opening sash).
- **Side-Guide Window-Mounted System** — reinforced top and bottom profiles with independent side guides rather than a complete four-sided surrounding frame (tensioned operation, guided by side rails, beading-bracket or adhesive installation).

Neither name is a supplier trademark, product name, or system name — both are neutral classification titles. See Decision Register for the reasoning; a mounting-method-as-Configuration-layer alternative was genuinely tested and rejected.

3. Blind Technology — a Separate Axis, Not a Construction Group

The inserted Blind Technology (Roller, Softshade, Pleated, Cellular, Dual-Fabric Pleated/Cellular, Aluminium Venetian, Wooden Venetian) is not itself a Construction Group under this document. It is a separate, controlled axis that activates the applicable rules from the existing MCD-01B-01 through MCD-01B-07 specifications. This relationship is documented structurally only, without drafting the Blind-Technology-specific rules themselves here — those belong in each variant specification:

```text
Blind Technology (activates the referenced specification's rules; drafted in each variant, not here)
├── Roller               → references MCD-01B-01
├── Vertical              → references MCD-01B-02
├── Roman                 → references MCD-01B-03
├── Wooden Venetian       → references MCD-01B-04
├── Faux Wood Venetian    → references MCD-01B-05
├── Aluminium Venetian    → references MCD-01B-06 (Metal Venetian)
├── Pleated / Cellular / Dual-Fabric Pleated/Cellular → references MCD-01B-07
└── Softshade              → no existing base specification yet; a future variant referencing this document alone, pending its own base specification
```

A Construction Group (§2) and a Blind Technology (this section) are independent, orthogonal selections — a given variant specification is defined by exactly one Construction Group and exactly one Blind Technology, not by treating Blind Technology as a sub-branch of Construction Group or vice versa. See Decision Register for confirmation of this classification and why it is not itself a Construction Group.

4. Exclusions

Explicitly excluded from this family and all its future variants: INTU; Micro INTU; roof-window systems; skylight systems; lantern-roof systems; shaped roof blinds; gable systems; specialist angled roof/side-window systems (a separate specialist product line, out of scope here — consistent with the standing project decision that Perfect Fit-equivalent specialist roof systems sit outside standard product families); ordinary free-hanging blinds; ordinary tensioned blinds outside this family; total-blackout cassette systems; any shutter-lite or shutter product (belongs under a future Shutters specification); standard recess-mounted or face-fixed Roller, Venetian, or Pleated blinds (already covered by their own existing specifications, MCD-01B-01 through MCD-01B-07). See Decision Register.

5. Organisational Role Schema (Schema Only — Not Populated in This Task)

Real evidence shows the supplier relationships in this product family are more complex than any prior product in this project — a single named organisation can hold multiple distinct roles simultaneously, and some roles exist with no active finished-goods route to Solara at all. This document defines, as a schema only, the distinct roles MCD-04 must be able to represent for this family. No real organisation name, product, or evidence is populated against this schema in this task — that happens only when each variant specification is drafted.

```text
Organisational Role Schema (MCD-04 schema definition — populated with real records only in future variant-drafting tasks)
├── System Owner
├── Patent Holder
├── Component Manufacturer
├── Licence Holder
├── Finished-Blind Manufacturer
├── Ordering Supplier
├── Reseller
├── Fabric Supplier
├── Commercial Route
└── Technical Source
```

A single organisation may hold several of these roles simultaneously for a given product route (e.g. an organisation that is both an Ordering Supplier and a Reseller of a different organisation's finished product), or none at all in an active commercial sense for a given route (e.g. a system inventor that licenses its technology to manufacturers without itself supplying finished goods to Solara — such an organisation may be recorded as System Owner and/or Patent Holder and/or Licence Holder without ever appearing as an Ordering Supplier, Reseller, or Commercial Route on any actual quotable record). See Decision Register for the Required Classification Question testing whether this richer model requires the Supplier Capability Engine to be Extended.

6. Shared Survey and Measurement Fields

Defined at the family level only, not tied to any specific Blind Technology:

- **Measurement basis** — e.g. glass size vs. supplier-specific product size. Do not enable Recess or Exact measurement automatically unless a future variant's supplier system explicitly uses them.
- **Handle clearance** — window-handle position, vent position, clearance to glass, obstruction conflict.
- **Bead and bracket survey** — bead type/profile, bead depth, rubber bead protrusion, bracket compatibility, frame obstruction, handle obstruction, seal condition, window material, opening type.
- **Frame group and frame colour** — Configuration-layer fields, supplier-capability controlled. Do not create one universal colour list; colour and frame-group availability will always be filtered by the exact variant/supplier product once populated.
- **Fixing method** — Glazing-Bead Bracket / Beading-Bracket Side Guide / Adhesive Side Guide, supplier-capability controlled.

7. Validation Principles (Family Level)

Documented as family-wide validation principles, without populating specific numeric limits — those belong to each variant's supplier capability records:

- A product can only be quoted when Construction Group, exact supplier system, size, area, frame group, frame colour, bracket/fixing method, operation, fabric/slat compatibility, control, and window compatibility all match a confirmed supplier capability record.
- No cross-supplier inheritance: sizes, bracket depths, component capability, frame colours, motorisation, and fabric limits must never be inherited from one supplier's evidence into another supplier's or another product's record.
- Pricing-grid values (first/last entries in a price table) are not automatically technical limits — store "Minimum/Maximum Priced Band" separately from "Technical Minimum/Maximum," and only populate the technical fields where a specification explicitly confirms them.
- Where a supplier states that a dimensional limit depends on fabric thickness or fabric choice, require fabric selection before final validation and apply the fabric-specific limit where available; otherwise mark "Supplier Confirmation Required" rather than allowing the advisor to assume a table maximum.

8. Motorisation Principle (Family Level)

Motorisation is not enabled globally across this family. A manufacturing-system-level motor capability existing at the system-owner level does not by itself establish an active quoting route — quoting must remain blocked until a specific supplier's specific product is confirmed to support motorisation. This mirrors the Category-Level Availability Confirmed / Quoting Status Disabled distinction already established for Metal Venetian (MCD-01B-06 §14): evidence that motorisation exists at a general or system-ownership level is not treated as equivalent to a confirmed, quotable capability. The specific system name and evidence belong in MCD-04 when the relevant variant is drafted, not in this document.

9. Child Safety (Family Level)

The existing Child Safety Shared Service applies. Not every framed or window-mounted system is automatically Safe by Design — classification depends on the exact control mechanism of the inserted Blind Technology: handle-operated tensioned systems may be Safe by Design where evidence confirms this; chain-operated systems require the appropriate safety device; wand and cord systems require applicable controls. The specific classification per Blind Technology and supplier product happens in each variant specification, using the Child Safety Engine treatment already established in the relevant base specification (MCD-01B-01 through MCD-01B-07).

10. Customer-Document and Audit Rules

The existing project-wide rules apply. Customer documents show: product description; fabric/slat and colour; frame colour; operation; total price; lead time; and warranty where applicable. Customer documents must not show: internal component codes; supplier costs; reseller relationships; manufacturing deductions; bracket calculations; measurements; technical evidence statuses; or internal capability notes.

The audit record must preserve: ordering supplier; finished-blind manufacturer; system owner; exact supplier system/product; Construction Group; Blind Technology; fabric/slat details; frame details; bracket/fixing selection; operation; control side; applied limits; source document/version; advisor overrides; supplier-confirmation items; and pricing/reseller route where applicable.
