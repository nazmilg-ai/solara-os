## 3. Document classification

MCD-01B-08A is a Product Specification. It inherits family architecture from **MCD-01B-08 — Frame-Mounted and Window-Mounted Blind Systems**, specifically its already-ratified **Side-Guide Window-Mounted System** Construction Group. It also inherits base product behaviour from **MCD-01B-07 — Pleated & Cellular Blinds**.

It must record only the product-specific behaviour created by the side-guide window-mounted construction. It must not duplicate the complete Pleated/Cellular engine already established in MCD-01B-07.

## 4. Product-family position

Place this specification under:

```
Frame-Mounted and Window-Mounted Blind Systems (MCD-01B-08)
→ Side-Guide Window-Mounted System
  → Pleated & Cellular variant (this document)
```

Do not introduce "Bi-Fold Door Systems" as a new Product Family or Construction Group. Bi-fold doors, patio doors, tilt-and-turn windows, and suitable standard windows are **application contexts** — a field this specification captures and validates against, not an architectural family.

Supported application contexts must include, subject to supplier capability and survey validation: bi-fold doors; patio doors; tilt-and-turn windows; suitable standard windows; compatible low-profile glazing arrangements. Do not state that it is suitable for every bi-fold door, patio door, or low-profile window — compatibility remains dependent on frame/bead profile, rubber-gasket profile and condition, fixing route, handle clearance, opening/closing/folding clearance, dimensions, and current supplier capability.

## 5. Product definition

Define this product as a screw-free, window-mounted blind system using: reinforced top profile; reinforced bottom profile; separate side guides; tensioned blind construction; bead-mounted or adhesive-mounted installation routes.

It is not: a complete joined perimeter frame (that is the Full-Frame Bead-Mounted System, a different Construction Group under MCD-01B-08); a conventional recess- or face-fixed blind; a total-blackout cassette system; an INTU or Micro INTU system.

The underlying supplier specification confirms reinforced top and bottom profiles, separate side rails, Top Down Bottom Up operation, and two installation routes.

## 6. Confirmed Blind Technologies — this work package

Support only: (1) Pleated; (2) Cellular.

Aluminium Venetian is confirmed to exist under this construction from supplier evidence but is explicitly out of scope for this work package — it is reserved for a separate, later specification (MCD-01B-08B), because it inherits from a materially different base specification (MCD-01B-06) and has different product behaviour, operation, controls, measurement limits, clearance rules, material configuration, accessories, child-safety analysis, and supplier capabilities. Do not draft any Aluminium Venetian content in this document.

Do not add: Roller; Wooden Venetian; Faux Wood Venetian; Softshade; striped Roller Night & Day; shutters; roof or gable systems.

## 7. Construction and operation

Confirmed: tensioned construction; Top Down Bottom Up operation; guided by side rails; handle or no-handle control options; reinforced top and bottom profiles.

Do not use "Night & Day" as the neutral technology name. Where two fabrics are supported, use the established neutral terminology from MCD-01B-07: **Dual-Fabric Pleated/Cellular**. Do not assume Dual-Fabric availability for this construction unless supplier evidence confirms it.

### 7.1 Operation mode

The current evidence supports manual operation. Motorisation must be recorded as **Not Provided** unless a current supplier-issued specification confirming motorisation for this specific construction is found. Do not infer motorisation from Pleated, Cellular, or other framed systems.

## 8. Measurement basis

The ordering measurement basis is **Visible Glass Size**. Required capture: glass width at top, middle, and bottom; smallest width used; glass drop at left, centre, and right; smallest drop used; do not include bead or rubber seals in the measurement. The measuring guide confirms visible-glass measurement and smallest-of-three ordering.

**Required architectural test:** determine whether Visible Glass Size is an existing configured Measurement Engine reference-point mode or requires an extension to the shared Measurement Engine. Apply MCD-01A's objective Extended test: Extended only where the shared platform service itself requires new behaviour. Record full reasoning in the Decision Register.

## 9. Frame, bead, and gasket assessment

### 9.1 Required survey fields

Frame/bead profile: Square; Chamfered; Ovolo/Curved; Other; Unknown.
Rubber-gasket condition: Flush; Protruding; Thick/Raised; Recessed; Damaged/Distorted; Other; Unknown.
Additional fields: gasket protrusion onto glass; available flat seating surface; bead/fixing compatibility; fixing route; handle clearance; other obstruction clearance; opening clearance; door folding/stacking clearance; photographs where the workflow supports them; advisor notes.

### 9.2 Square-profile interpretation

Record this distinction carefully. Supplier wording: "On square frame windows, please ensure there is a minimum of 2mm rubber bead protruding onto the glass." Operational interpretation: this phrase refers to a square-*profile* frame/bead and rubber-gasket arrangement, not the overall rectangular shape of the window. This interpretation is supported by a comparative supplier's survey model for a different framed system, which separately asks the surveyor to check frame and rubber-seal type and whether the square profile has a flush rubber seal — cite that comparative source generically in this document (do not name the comparative supplier here; the comparative source name belongs in MCD-04) as supporting evidence, not as the defining source of this supplier's own capability.

Record the interpretation as: **Confirmed Operational Supplier Knowledge — supported by comparative supplier evidence.** Do not misstate it as "Minimum bead depth: 2mm" — the 2mm value is rubber-gasket protrusion onto the glass, not bead depth.

### 9.3 Initial compatibility policy

Pending independent review and ratification:

Eligible by default: square-profile bead/frame; minimum 2mm rubber-gasket protrusion onto glass; gasket capable of allowing the brackets or side guides to seat correctly; all other clearances passed.

Not automatically eligible: chamfered bead; ovolo or curved bead; unknown profile; thick, raised, or distorted gasket; unsuitable or insufficient seating surface. These conditions must trigger: **Physical Assessment or Supplier Confirmation Required**. Do not describe non-square profiles as universally impossible unless the supplier confirms that exclusion.

**Required judgment call:** determine whether the proposed square-profile eligibility policy is a confirmed product rule, a conservative operational restriction, or an unresolved supplier-capability question. Record the conclusion and reasoning in the Decision Register as pending ratification.

## 10. Fixing routes

Support two supplier-configured fixing routes: (1) Beading Clip; (2) High-Strength Adhesive Tape. The supplier documentation confirms beading brackets connecting into the side guide, and adhesive tape affixing the side guide to the window. Do not assume both fixing routes are valid for every profile. The Supplier Capability record must determine eligible frame/bead profiles; gasket conditions; required flat mounting surface; bracket/component allocation; restrictions; current availability.

Exact supplier codes belong in MCD-04, not the neutral operative rules. Confirmed examples include codes for the Pleated/Cellular construction (see §23 for the required source documents). Do not duplicate those codes throughout MCD-01B-08A.md.

## 11. Handle and obstruction clearance

The supplier measuring guide states: the handle should be at least 35mm from the depth/plane of the window glass. Implement: glass-to-handle distance; other obstruction distance; opening/closing test; folding-panel collision test where relevant. Minimum confirmed handle clearance: **35mm**. Block or hold the configuration where the confirmed clearance is not met.

## 12. Confirmed dimensional limits

Confirmed: minimum width 200mm; maximum width 1,500mm; minimum drop 150mm; maximum drop 2,300mm; maximum area 3m². Linked restrictions: 1,500mm width is available only up to 2,000mm drop; 2,300mm drop is available only up to 1,300mm width.

## 13. Fabric and material rules

Inherit neutral material and fabric classifications from MCD-01B-07. Supplier-specific availability belongs in MCD-04.

## 14. Product-selection flow

The advisor workflow should follow: Product Family and Construction Group (inherited from MCD-01B-08) → Application Type → Blind Technology (Pleated / Cellular) → Ordering Supplier → Exact Supplier Product → Visible-Glass Measurements → Frame/Bead Profile → Rubber-Gasket Condition → Gasket Protrusion → Handle/Obstruction Clearance → Fixing Route → Fabric Collection → Fabric → Colourway → Profile Colour → Control/Handle Options → Accessories → Validation → Price.

Note: displaying the real ordering supplier and exact supplier product name to the advisor during configuration is a normal, already-established workflow behaviour (matching how customer-facing documents may show "exact visible product terminology where commercially appropriate") — this is distinct from, and does not conflict with, §1.5's rule that the document's own title and neutral architecture must not use the supplier trademark.

## 15. Advisor-first behaviour

The application must guide rather than obstruct unnecessarily. It should: show supplier-supported choices only; explain why a profile is not automatically eligible; permit the advisor to record an unresolved physical assessment; preserve photographs and notes; show valid alternative products where this construction is unsuitable; never silently substitute Perfect Fit or INTU; block final ordering only where a mandatory technical or supplier rule fails.

The advisor must not override: confirmed dimensional limits; maximum area; mandatory clearance; unsupported supplier capability; incompatible fixing route; mandatory gasket/profile rule once ratified.

## 16. Recommendation rules

The Recommendation Engine may recommend this construction where: a compatible bi-fold, patio-door, tilt-and-turn, or window profile is present; the customer wants a screw-free solution; conventional full-frame bead-depth requirements cannot be met; the profile and gasket rules pass. The system must explain why this construction may be more suitable, and that suitability must be confirmed by survey. Do not claim it is universally the replacement for every failed full-frame application. Where unsuitable, the Recommendation Engine may surface other supported systems without automatically selecting them.

## 17. Child safety

Do not assume it is automatically child-safe, cord-free, or that ordinary Pleated/Cellular safety devices apply unchanged. Record: control method; presence or absence of accessible cords; required safety device; installation compliance; refusal workflow applicability; source evidence. Supplier-specific child-safety claims remain in MCD-04.
