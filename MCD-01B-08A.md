## Documentation Conventions: Advisor Selection / Derived Default / Supplier Capability

Consistent with every other product specification in this project, the following three terms are used throughout this document as **documentation conventions only** — they are not new architectural layers and do not modify MCD-01A §3:

- **Advisor Selection** — a value the advisor actively chooses. Architecturally, this is the Configuration layer (or, where the choice changes construction, the Construction/System layer).
- **Derived Default** — a value the system determines automatically from an Advisor Selection, without a separate question. Architecturally, this is the Accessory Engine and/or Supplier Capability Engine resolving a compatible component or value from an existing Configuration selection.
- **Supplier Capability** — the actual data (dimensions, weights, compatibility, pricing, availability) that makes a Derived Default resolvable. Architecturally, this is the Supplier Capability Engine (MCD-01A §5.9) and the Supplier Capability Library, maintained in MCD-04.

1. Product Definition

This product is a screw-free, window-mounted blind system using: reinforced top profile; reinforced bottom profile; separate side guides; tensioned blind construction; bead-mounted or adhesive-mounted installation routes.

It is not: a complete joined perimeter frame (that is the Full-Frame Bead-Mounted System, a different Construction Group under MCD-01B-08); a conventional recess- or face-fixed blind; a total-blackout cassette system; an INTU or Micro INTU system (a comparative, separately governed system — see Decision Register).

The underlying supplier specification confirms reinforced top and bottom profiles, separate side rails, Top Down Bottom Up operation, and two installation routes.

6. Measurement Basis

The ordering measurement basis is **Visible Glass Size**. Required capture: glass width at top, middle, and bottom; smallest width used; glass drop at left, centre, and right; smallest drop used; bead and rubber seals are not included in the measurement. The measuring guide confirms visible-glass measurement and smallest-of-three ordering. See Decision Register (DR-06) for the Configured-versus-Extended Measurement Engine reasoning.

This is a single, neutral measurement concept. Any supplier-specific ordering field names used to capture or transmit it belong in MCD-04 as an external supplier mapping, not in this document, and must not replace this document's own neutral platform terminology anywhere in this document. This construction's own supplier evidence (MCD-04 §7.15) confirms one ordering method only; it does not establish a second, gasket-condition-selected ordering method — see Decision Register (DR-07) for why a comparable finding, present in the sibling Aluminium Venetian variant (MCD-01B-08B), is not adopted here.

7. Frame, Bead, and Gasket Assessment

7.1 Required survey fields

Frame/bead profile: Square; Chamfered; Ovolo/Curved; Other; Unknown.
Rubber-gasket condition: Flush; Protruding; Thick/Raised; Recessed; Damaged/Distorted; Other; Unknown.
Additional fields: gasket protrusion onto glass; available flat seating surface; bead/fixing compatibility; fixing route; handle clearance; other obstruction clearance; opening clearance; door folding/stacking clearance; photographs where the workflow supports them; advisor notes.

7.2 Square-profile interpretation

Supplier wording: "On square frame windows, please ensure there is a minimum of 2mm rubber bead protruding onto the glass." Operational interpretation: this phrase refers to a square-*profile* frame/bead and rubber-gasket arrangement, not the overall rectangular shape of the window. This interpretation is supported by a comparative supplier's survey model for a different framed system (an INTU-branded comparative system — cited here only as a comparative evidence category, not as the defining source of this supplier's own capability; the comparative supplier's own name is confined to MCD-04's citation, not used generically here), which separately asks the surveyor to check frame and rubber-seal type and whether the square profile has a flush rubber seal.

Recorded as: **Confirmed Operational Supplier Knowledge — supported by comparative supplier evidence.** This is not restated as "Minimum bead depth: 2mm" — the 2mm value is rubber-gasket protrusion onto the glass, not bead depth. See Decision Register (DR-07).

7.3 Initial compatibility policy

Pending independent review and ratification:

Eligible by default: square-profile bead/frame; minimum 2mm rubber-gasket protrusion onto glass; gasket capable of allowing the brackets or side guides to seat correctly; all other clearances passed.

Not automatically eligible: chamfered bead; ovolo or curved bead; unknown profile; thick, raised, or distorted gasket; unsuitable or insufficient seating surface. These conditions trigger: **Physical Assessment or Supplier Confirmation Required**. Non-square profiles are not described as universally impossible — no supplier confirmation of that exclusion has been found. See Decision Register (DR-07) for the required judgment call on whether this policy is a confirmed product rule, a conservative operational restriction, or an unresolved supplier-capability question.

8. Fixing Routes

Two supplier-configured fixing routes are supported: (1) Beading Clip; (2) High-Strength Adhesive Tape. The supplier documentation confirms beading brackets connecting into the side guide, and adhesive tape affixing the side guide to the window. Both fixing routes are not assumed valid for every profile — the Supplier Capability record determines eligible frame/bead profiles; gasket conditions; required flat mounting surface; bracket/component allocation; restrictions; current availability.

Exact supplier codes belong in MCD-04, not the neutral operative rules here — see MCD-04 for the confirmed codes for this construction. See Decision Register (DR-09) for the Configuration-versus-Construction reasoning on these two fixing routes.

9. Handle and Obstruction Clearance

The supplier measuring guide states: the handle should be at least 35mm from the depth/plane of the window glass. Implemented: glass-to-handle distance; other obstruction distance; opening/closing test; folding-panel collision test where relevant. Minimum confirmed handle clearance: **35mm**. The configuration is blocked or held where the confirmed clearance is not met.

10. Confirmed Dimensional Limits

Confirmed: minimum width 200mm; maximum width 1,500mm; minimum drop 150mm; maximum drop 2,300mm; maximum area 3m². Linked restrictions: 1,500mm width is available only up to 2,000mm drop; 2,300mm drop is available only up to 1,300mm width.

13. Advisor-First Behaviour

The application guides rather than obstructs unnecessarily. The advisor may not override: confirmed dimensional limits; maximum area; mandatory clearance; unsupported supplier capability; incompatible fixing route; mandatory gasket/profile rule once ratified.

14. Recommendation Rules

The Recommendation Engine may recommend this construction where: a compatible bi-fold, patio-door, tilt-and-turn, or window profile is present; the customer wants a screw-free solution; conventional full-frame bead-depth requirements cannot be met; the profile and gasket rules pass. The system explains why this construction may be more suitable, and that suitability must be confirmed by survey. This construction is not claimed to be universally the replacement for every failed full-frame application. Where unsuitable, the Recommendation Engine may surface other supported systems without automatically selecting them.
