# 3. Product Definition

A Day & Night Blind is a roller-based blind manufactured from a continuous fabric containing alternating horizontal transparent or sheer bands and opaque or denser bands.

The fabric passes through the roller system and around a lower section so that two layers of fabric are visible.

By moving one layer relative to the other, the user can:

* align the opaque bands for increased privacy;
* offset the bands to allow light through the sheer sections;
* raise the complete blind to expose the window.

The blind therefore has two distinct functions:

1. raising and lowering the complete blind;
2. adjusting the relative alignment of the front and rear fabric layers.

The fabric follows a fixed, system-specific route through the headrail, around the lower bar, and back into the system. This routing is determined by the manufacturing system and is not an advisor-selectable option.

# 4. Core Product Principles

## 4.1 Roller-based mechanism

Day & Night blinds belong to the roller-based product family.

They use the same core categories of:

* roller tube;
* clutch or motor;
* brackets;
* drive-end mechanism;
* pin-end mechanism;
* fascia or cassette;
* operating chain;
* tubular motor.

The system differs from a standard Roller Blind primarily because of:

* the fabric construction;
* the fixed fabric return path;
* the double-layer visual effect;
* the requirement for stripe or band alignment;
* the Day & Night bottom-bar arrangement;
* product-specific size and fabric limits.

## 4.2 Shared motor principle

Roller Blinds and Day & Night Blinds use the same supplier motor families where they use the same roller tube and mechanism. The motor catalogue should be shared, while finished-blind limits remain product-, fabric-, system- and supplier-specific.

The motor itself does not require a separate Day & Night product record.

The shared motor record may contain:

* supplier;
* motor brand;
* motor model;
* compatible tube diameter;
* torque;
* speed;
* minimum motor insertion width;
* power source;
* control type;
* remote compatibility;
* app compatibility;
* smart-home compatibility;
* warranty.

The finished blind must still be validated separately against Day & Night fabric, cassette and dimensional restrictions.

## 4.2a Motorisation Architecture

Motorisation must not be modelled as a simple finished-blind option such as "Manual blind" or "Factory-motorised blind." This distinction was proposed in an earlier revision of this document and is incorrect — it is withdrawn.

Across all suppliers, the blind system and its motorisation components are priced and ordered separately. Solara purchases the compatible motor, remote, charger, extension cables, hub, and other controls; installs the motor into the blind tube; and programmes, pairs, commissions, and tests the completed blind. This applies universally to both Roller and Day & Night blinds, across every supplier — there is no supplier-dependent "complete factory-motorised unit" path.

Motorisation is therefore built as a collection of compatible components, not a single product option:

```
Blind product
+ compatible tube/barrel
+ tubular motor
+ motor adaptor/crown and drive
+ remote or wall control
+ charger or power supply
+ extension cable, where required
+ hub/connectivity device, where required
+ installation
+ programming and commissioning
```

The absence of a motor pricing entry within a supplier's Day & Night pricing section does **not** indicate that motorisation is unavailable — the motor and its accessories may instead be priced in that supplier's Roller section, a wholesale components section, a dedicated motorisation section, or a separate motor price list. See §6 for the current supplier-specific position.

**Corrected governing rule**: Roller Blinds and Day & Night Blinds use compatible tubular motors fitted into their roller tubes. Suppliers generally provide wholesale prices for the motor and related controls or accessories separately from the blind price. Solara selects and purchases the appropriate components, installs the motor into the blind tube, programmes and pairs the controls, commissions the blind and tests its operation. The same supplier motor catalogue may serve both Roller and Day & Night blinds, subject to tube, system, dimensions, load and component compatibility.

### 7.2.1 Cruze fascia system — source figures

The Cruze 40 mm Open/70 mm Closed fascia system figures are given by three separate Decora Cruze specifications, which mostly agree with each other but conflict with a fourth on two fields:

* `SPEC72 - Softshade Specification - Issue V2 - 06.08.2025.pdf`, p. 2 ("CRUZE 40MM OPEN/70MM CLOSED FASCIA SYSTEMS SPECIFICATION"): width 200–2,500 mm; drop 200–3,000 mm; child safety D-Clip Chain Tidy; minimum control length 150 mm; deduction from floor 1,500 mm; tolerance ±3–5 mm.
* `SPEC75 - Premier Softshade Specification - Issue V3 - 20.08.2025.pdf`, p. 2 ("CRUZE 70MM CLOSED FASCIA SYSTEMS" — 70 mm variant only): width 200–2,500 mm; drop 200–3,000 mm; child safety D-Clip Chain Tidy; minimum control length 150 mm; deduction from floor 1,500 mm; tolerance ±3–5 mm.
* `SPEC76 - Cruze Softshade Wholesale Specification - Issue V2 - 18.02.2026.pdf`, p. 2 ("CRUZE 40MM OPEN/70MM CLOSED FASCIA SYSTEMS"): minimum width 200 mm, minimum drop 200 mm; maximum width 2,500 mm, maximum drop 3,000 mm. (Child safety and minimum control length are not stated on this page.)
* `SPEC71 - Prime Softshade Specification - Issue V5 - 17.12.2025.pdf`, p. 4 ("CRUZE 40MM OPEN/70MM CLOSED FASCIA SYSTEMS"): width 200–2,500 mm; **drop 500–3,000 mm**; child safety Tie Down Clip; **minimum control length 300 mm**; deduction from floor 1,500 mm; tolerance ±3–5 mm.

**Unresolved source conflict, recorded rather than silently resolved:** SPEC72, SPEC75 and SPEC76 agree on a 200 mm minimum drop for the Cruze system; SPEC71 states 500 mm. SPEC72 and SPEC75 agree on a 150 mm minimum control length and D-Clip Chain Tidy child safety; SPEC71 states 300 mm and Tie Down Clip; SPEC76 states neither field. This is a genuine disagreement between current Decora Cruze specifications, not a transcription error by this document, and is not resolved by majority vote alone — see the Decision Register (§30, DR-01) for how it is being handled operationally.

### 7.2.2 Louvolite fascia system — source figures

* `SPEC72 - Softshade Specification - Issue V2 - 06.08.2025.pdf`, p. 4 ("LOUVOLITE 40MM OPEN FASCIA/70MM CLOSED FASCIA SYSTEMS SPECIFICATION"): System Type Standard = 70 mm Open Fascia, Optional Variation = 40 mm Closed Fascia; width 300–2,500 mm; drop 500–3,000 mm; child safety Tie Down Clip; minimum control length 300 mm; deduction from floor 1,500 mm; clutches "Louvolite 32 mm"/"Louvolite 40 mm"; finish "Vision Bottom Bar"; tolerance ±3–5 mm; footnote: "40mm Closed Fascia available in Reverse only." This is currently the sole located Louvolite source for this system.

### 7.2.3 Solara operating figures — most-generous-of-two-systems policy

Solara will offer both the Cruze and Louvolite fascia systems for this Semi-Cassette configuration. Where the two systems' real figures differ, the following operating figures use **the more generous of the two** — this is a deliberate Solara business decision, not a single-source technical fact, and must not be read or cited as if one supplier document stated all of these values together:

* **minimum width: 200 mm** (Cruze, §7.2.1) — more generous than Louvolite's 300 mm (§7.2.2);
* **minimum drop: 200 mm** (Cruze, per SPEC72/SPEC75/SPEC76 — see the unresolved conflict with SPEC71 above) — more generous than Louvolite's 500 mm;
* **maximum drop: 3,000 mm** — identical across Cruze and Louvolite, not a "most generous" pick;
* **maximum width: 2,500 mm** — identical across Cruze and Louvolite, not a "most generous" pick;
* **minimum control length: 150 mm** (Cruze, per SPEC72/SPEC75) — more generous than Louvolite's 300 mm;
* **deduction from floor: 1,500 mm** — identical across Cruze and Louvolite, not a "most generous" pick;
* **tolerance: approximately ±3–5 mm** — consistent across all four source documents.

**Child safety device is not a "most generous" field.** Cruze's device is D-Clip Chain Tidy (per SPEC72/SPEC75 — SPEC71 states Tie Down Clip for the same system; see the unresolved conflict above) and Louvolite's is Tie Down Clip (§7.2.2). These are two different physical device types, not two values on a scale, so this document records both by system rather than selecting one — the advisor-facing configuration must resolve the correct device from the selected fascia system (Cruze or Louvolite), not from a single blended Semi-Cassette default. This gap was not addressed by the "most generous" policy instruction and is flagged here rather than resolved by assumption.

The product engine must apply these Semi-Cassette figures per the selected fascia system (Cruze or Louvolite) where the underlying system-specific values are actually needed (e.g. child safety, and the Cruze/SPEC71 drop-minimum and control-length conflict above); the combined "most generous" figures above are the Solara-facing operating envelope for this Semi-Cassette configuration as a whole.

# 10. Fabric Selection

## 10.1 Fabric structure

Day & Night fabric contains alternating:

* sheer or transparent horizontal bands;
* opaque, denser or decorative horizontal bands.

The appearance and privacy level depend on how the two fabric layers align.

## 10.2 Fabric categories

Supplier collections may include:

* plain opaque bands with sheer voile;
* textured opaque bands with sheer voile;
* patterned bands;
* wood-look bands;
* metallic or special-effect bands.

**No blackout fabric option exists for Day & Night.** Day & Night fabric is a sheer construction — a continuous fabric with sheer/transparent bands alternating with opaque or denser bands (§10.1, §3) — and cannot be a blackout material. An earlier revision of this section incorrectly listed "blackout-material bands with sheer voile" as an available fabric category; this was a direct contradiction of §10.3's own "not a total-blackout blind" rule and of this document's exclusion of total-blackout cassette systems (§1), and has been removed rather than left standing alongside the correct rule. This is consistent with, and does not narrow, the existing distinction in §10.3 between a supplier's "blackout"-labelled *opaque band* material and the complete blind's actual (non-total-blackout) light performance — the fabric category itself is never blackout for this product.

These descriptions refer to the fabric construction and not necessarily the performance of the complete blind.

## 10.3 Blackout terminology

A Day & Night Blind must not be described as a total-blackout blind.

Even where a supplier describes the opaque band as "blackout", light may still enter through:

* sheer sections;
* incomplete stripe alignment;
* side gaps;
* bottom gaps;
* bracket gaps;
* cassette gaps;
* fabric-to-hardware gaps.

The customer-facing explanation should state:

> Aligning the opaque bands increases privacy and reduces light, but a Day & Night Blind is not a total-blackout system.

## 10.4 Privacy limitation

When the sheer bands are aligned, visibility through the blind may increase.

The advisor must explain that:

* daytime privacy depends on internal and external light levels;
* evening privacy may reduce when lights are on inside;
* the blind should be moved to the more closed alignment for increased privacy;
* privacy performance varies by fabric colour and weave.

## 10.5 Fabric viewing rule

Fabric samples should be held against the actual window during the consultation where practical.

The customer should not rely only on the appearance of the sample in the sample book because:

* daylight changes fabric colour;
* sheer sections appear differently against glass;
* external brightness affects transparency;
* darker rooms can change perceived colour;
* privacy performance varies with lighting conditions.

## 10.6 Fabric joins

**Resolved — fabric joins are not available for Day & Night.** Day & Night fabric is a sheer construction with a fixed, system-specific alternating-band route through the roller, cassette/fascia, and lower bar (§3, §10.1); it cannot be joined. This closes the previously open evidence item recorded in §27 and §29.

If the selected finished size exceeds the usable fabric width, the configuration must be:

* blocked;
* referred to supplier;
* or changed to multiple blinds.
